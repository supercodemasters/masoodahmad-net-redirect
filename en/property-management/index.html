Industries

IT and software for property owners and managers

Fault reports, tenants and maintenance plan in the same system.

Property owners and managers handle fault reports, tenants, contracts and maintenance. When fault reports arrive by phone and the maintenance plan lives in Excel, the overview is lost.

Common challenges

  • Fault reports by phone and email
  • A maintenance plan in a spreadsheet
  • Tenants ask for the same information

What can be built

  • Web fault reporting with status for the tenant
  • A maintenance plan and history per property
  • A tenant portal with documents and news
  • A dashboard for vacancies, cases and costs

Frequently asked questions

Can tenants follow their case?

Yes. The tenant sees the status and is told when the case is closed.

Does it work for a small manager?

Yes. It scales from a few to many properties.
