Industries

IT and software for waste and recycling companies

Collections, weights and reports without manual compilation.

Companies in waste, recycling and water handle many collections, weights and reports. The data often has to be compiled for customers and authorities, which takes time when done by hand.

Common challenges

  • Collections are planned in spreadsheets
  • Weights and volumes are compiled manually
  • Customer reports take a long time

What can be built

  • Planning of collections per customer and area
  • Recording of weights and fractions on mobile
  • Automatic reports per customer and period
  • A customer portal with history and invoices

Frequently asked questions

Can the driver record on mobile?

Yes. Collection, weight and deviations are recorded on the spot.

Can reports be adapted per customer?

Yes. Each customer can get their own report with their fractions and periods.
