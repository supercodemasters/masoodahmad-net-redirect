Industries

IT and software for construction and civil engineering firms

Projects, time and changes documented on site, straight on mobile.

Builders and civil engineering firms lose money on change orders that are not documented, time reported late and missing records at invoicing. A simple app on site makes a big difference.

Common challenges

  • Change orders are not documented in time
  • Timesheets come in late
  • Hard to see project finances during the work

What can be built

  • A project app with site diary, photos and change orders
  • Time reporting per project on mobile
  • Records straight to invoicing
  • A dashboard for project finances

Relevant experience

  • Project, staff and inventory control with approval flows and offline mode (Uzmar, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for subcontractors?

Yes. They can get their own login with access only to their parts.

Can photos be linked to change orders?

Yes. Photos, time and description are saved together and can be sent to the client.
