Industries

IT and software for farms and forestry companies

Less administration, more time outside.

Farms, forestry companies and fisheries have more and more administration: documentation, grant applications, machines and often their own sales. Simple systems can remove much of the paperwork.

Common challenges

  • Documentation spread across binders and apps
  • Farm sales handled by phone and text
  • Machines and service tracked from memory

What can be built

  • Ordering and payment for farm sales
  • A register of machines, service and costs
  • Gathered documentation per field or stand
  • Simple reports for advisers and accounting

Frequently asked questions

Is it too big for a family business?

No. We start with the part that takes most time, such as farm sales.

Does it work on mobile outdoors?

Yes. Everything is built for mobile and can work offline.
